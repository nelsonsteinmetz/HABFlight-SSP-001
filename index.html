Altitude

BMP180 (blue) assumes a standard atmosphere and diverges above ~9 km; GPS (orange) is QNH-independent and accurate end-to-end. Background bands: ASCENT (green), BURST (orange), DESCENT (blue). The slope at any point is the vertical rate — see the Ascent / descent rate plot for a smoothed numeric view.

Ascent / descent rate

Vertical speed smoothed with a 60 s rolling median. Typical HAB: steady 5-7 m/s ascent, sharp dive to 20-30 m/s right after burst (parachute barely catches in near-vacuum), then gradual slowing as the chute bites in thicker air.

Temperature

Inside-payload temperature. Follows the ambient profile: warm near the ground, cold trough through the tropopause (−40 to −55 °C around 10-15 km), slight warming climbing into the stratosphere.

Pressure

Raw BMP180 pressure. Falls roughly exponentially — half of sea-level at ~5.5 km, 10 % at 16 km, below 1 % above 30 km. BMP180's range runs out around 9 km, so values above are noisy and shouldn't be trusted.

Ground track

Flight path on ESRI World Imagery, coloured by altitude (km). Green = launch, red = last fix. Top-5 nearby cities labelled for scale; the altitude colourbar is on the right.

3D trajectory over satellite floor

3D flight path with ESRI imagery as the ground plane. XY = km east/north from first fix, Z = altitude (km). Animation sweeps 90° and back every 4 s; a static PNG and a 10 s 60 fps MP4 sit alongside.

GPS satellites

Satellites used in the position solution. 6+ is the sketch's 'good fix' threshold; more generally means tighter accuracy. Sudden drops to 0 point to antenna shadowing, EMI, or fast rotation masking the sky.

Sample interval

Time between consecutive CSV rows. Target is 1000 ms. Small spikes = SD write latency; sustained drift = SoftwareSerial buffer pressure. Values above ~1050 ms mean a sample was delayed but not dropped.

State machine progression

Flight state 0-12. 0 = WAITING, 1 = ARMED_GROUND, 2-7 = ASCENT bands, 8 = BURST, 9-11 = DESCENT bands, 12 = LANDED. Smooth stairway = clean mission; sawtooth = jumper toggles or burst-detection oscillation.
